Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026. This explains what happens to the information you send through jetwithmatt.com.

Who this covers

Jet with Matt is the personal website of Matt Caruso, a private jet charter broker in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. Matt arranges flights through jets.com (Dem Jet Inc dba Jets.com). Flights are operated by FAA Part 135 certificated air carriers.

What I collect

When you fill out a form on this site, I collect what you enter: your name, email, phone number, trip details (route, dates, passengers, aircraft preference), how you currently fly, and anything you write in the notes. I don't ask for payment details, ID numbers, or passport information on this site.

The hosting provider may also log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit, for security and performance.

How I use it

  • To respond to your request and send you flight or jet card options.
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your request and related trips, if you've agreed to that on the form.
  • To keep a record of your trips and preferences so future requests go faster.

Who I share it with

I share your information only as needed to arrange your travel or run this site:

  • Jets.com, the charter broker I work with, to quote and book your flight or jet card.
  • Aircraft operators and service providers (such as catering or ground transportation) that need details to complete your trip.
  • Service providers that run this site: Vercel (hosting) and Formspree (form delivery).
  • Authorities, if required by law.

I don't sell your personal information.

Texts and calls

If you give a phone number and agree on the form, I may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or email me and I'll stop. Agreeing is not a condition of booking.

How long I keep it

I keep your information while we're working together and for as long as needed for business, tax, and legal records. You can ask me to delete it at any time, subject to records I'm required to keep.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information you've sent, or ask me to stop contacting you. Email jetwithmatt@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
