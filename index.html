Why Mac Jets

A leaner way to fly private.

The charter industry is built on layers. Mac Jets cuts through them. You get the same aircraft, sourced from the same operators, at a price that reflects what the plane actually costs to fly.

i.
Direct sourcing

No call centers, no junior brokers, no handoffs. You work with me from quote to wheels-down — the same person who answers when you text at 11pm.

ii.
Transparent pricing

Quotes itemized line by line: aircraft cost, fuel, FET, segment tax, brokerage fee. No mystery markups, no surprise add-ons at billing.

iii.
Same fleet, less margin

I source from the exact operators the major brokerages use. The difference is what gets stacked on top — I keep margins lean and pass it through.

The process

From request to runway in four steps.

01 —
You send the trip

Route, dates, passengers, anything special — bags, pets, catering. A text or email is enough.

02 —
I source options

Within hours you get 2–3 vetted aircraft options across categories, with all-in pricing and operator details.

03 —
You choose, I lock it

Pick the aircraft. Contract signed, plane secured, deposit handled — usually same day.

04 —
Door to door

Catering, ground transport, FBO coordination, crew briefing — handled. You show up. We fly.
