Fly often? Ask about a jet card.

A jet card lets you prepay for flying and book your trips against it, instead of starting from scratch on every flight. Terms differ by program, so I walk you through exactly what's included before you commit.

Ask about a jet card

Charter, trip by trip

Best if you fly now and then, want a different aircraft for each trip, or prefer no upfront commitment.

Jet card

Best if you fly regularly, want a prepaid program, and like simple repeat booking.

Not sure which fits? Tell me how often you fly and where, and I'll tell you which one makes sense.

Questions people ask.

How do you choose the aircraft?

We start with the route, passenger count, luggage, and what matters to you. I explain the cabin, range, and airport options so you can choose with the full picture.

Is a jet card always the better choice?

Not necessarily. It depends on how often you fly, your routes, your flexibility, and the program terms. I compare the options with you before you commit.

What if my plans change?

Contact me directly. I'll work through the options and explain any changes to availability or terms before you decide.

Do you operate the aircraft?

No. I arrange your flight, and it is operated by an FAA Part 135 certificated air carrier.
