Start with where you are, not just the airport code

I’m based in Fort Lauderdale. When you ask about a private flight from here, I want to know your departure address or general area, destination, preferred time, passengers, and luggage. That gives us a better starting point than choosing a jet first.

For the airport, we compare ground travel with the aircraft options for your trip. The closest airport is worth considering, but it is not the only factor. The operator must confirm that the aircraft and airport work for the itinerary.

An FBO is the private aviation facility used for services such as passenger handling and aircraft fueling. Your trip confirmation should name the actual facility and address. Do not head to an airline terminal unless that is specifically part of your instructions.

Choose the aircraft around the people and luggage

Passenger count is only the beginning. Tell me about large suitcases, golf clubs, strollers, pets, and any cabin needs. A seat count does not tell you whether everything comfortably fits.

Then we compare the route, cabin, luggage capacity, and whether the proposed aircraft can make the trip as planned. Any fuel stop or operational limitation needs to be part of that conversation. The operating carrier confirms the flight’s operational details.

For a short business trip, convenience may be the priority. For a longer family flight, cabin room and luggage may matter more. You do not need to know the model names to explain what you need.

What I need to plan a departure

  • Route: cities or airports, plus your destination area if you want help comparing airports.
  • Schedule: departure date and time, return date, extra stops, and how flexible you are.
  • Passengers: total count and any needs that affect boarding or the cabin.
  • Luggage: the number and size of bags, plus oversized items.
  • Extras: pets, catering preferences, and any ground transportation request.
  • International travel: tell me early so documentation and arrival arrangements can be reviewed for the itinerary.

Before departure, confirm the facility address, arrival instructions, passenger details, luggage arrangements, and the operating carrier. Keep your point of contact handy if the itinerary changes.
